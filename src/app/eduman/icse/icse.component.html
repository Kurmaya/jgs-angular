IN-DEPTH INFO ABOUT THE CURRICULUMS

ICSE

A comprehensive, well-structured syllabus designed by the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations, the ICSE curriculum aims to provide practical knowledge and build analytical skills in students. The curriculum’s ethos is: minimum monitoring, freedom to experiment with ideas, and students evolving their own niche. It focuses on the child’s overall growth by giving equal importance to all subjects. With an emphasis on the detailed study of each subject with an edge over English, this curriculum also offers the flexibility of selecting subjects that the students like and want to study.

IBDP

The International Baccalaureate® (IB) Diploma Programme (DP) is highly respected and widely recognized by leading universities across the globe. For students, success in the IB often translates into advanced standing, course credits, scholarships, and other admissions benefits at many prestigious institutions. IB graduates are exceptionally well-prepared to meet the demands of the 21st century and enjoy significantly higher acceptance rates at top universities worldwide.

At Johnson IBDP , our mission is to nurture each student with the aptitude, compassion, values, and skills needed to discover their interests and maximize their strengths.

Students benefit from a holistic learning journey that extends beyond the classroom. Along with rigorous academics, they participate in interactive classroom experiences, various club activities, service initiatives, and research-based learning opportunities, all of which enhance their career and life prospects.

The IB curriculum is rigorous, stimulating, and demanding, requiring consistent commitment and a growth mindset. At Johnson IBDP, every student receives personalized attention and continuous support to ensure a smooth and successful transition into the programme. Our dedicated and IB-trained teachers guide, mentor, and encourage students throughout their two-year journey, empowering them to graduate with confidence and secure placements in suitable and fulfilling career pathways.
