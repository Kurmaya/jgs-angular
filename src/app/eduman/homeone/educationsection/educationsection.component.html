A message from our Chairman

Dr B Chandra Sekhar

Chairman

Our Chairman Dr B Chandra Sekhar started his journey in the field of education in the year 1983. Being a true visionary at heart and believer in the philosophy of ‘We=Success’, trust is the biggest asset we’ve built up at the core of our organisation over the years. He firmly believes that the organisation’s success is attributed to teamwork rather than a one-person show. And that a shared vision creates unity and loyalty among all the team members leading to the feeling of credibility internally and externally.
