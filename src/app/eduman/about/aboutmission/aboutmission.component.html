Story

Starting back in 1979, we’ve come a long way. We have successfully completed 47+ years of providing stellar facilities and opportunities for students to learn and grow. With a vision to bring the best of global education to the country, we worked continuously to make Johnson what it is today. Johnson was the first institution to get ISO certification in the year 1998.

Dr B.Chandra Sekhar, an educator at his core, has always wanted to bring the best of global education to the school community. Founded by his passion and with 14 years of providing the IBDP curriculum, we are now at the Mallapur branch, now we are broadening our reach and starting our latest Kokapet branch. Given our rich history, we are one of the top schools in Kokapet.
