24:2 Student-teacher ratio

Excellent Student-teacher ratio

But our 3 Campuses, 500+ Educators, 7000+ Current Learners, and 20,000+ Alumni have always answered this issue logically. We provide an excellent student-teacher ratio in all classrooms. We make sure that every classroom has 2 facilitators.

Global Standards

One of the greatest strengths of education is that it knows no boundaries. Learning can happen anywhere, from anyone, and at any stage of life. As India evolved and the world became increasingly interconnected, it became essential to provide future generations with an education that met global standards while remaining rooted in strong values. We began this journey with the ICSE curriculum and later expanded to include the ISC and IB Diploma Programme (IBDP), offering students internationally benchmarked learning opportunities. Continuing this vision, we established Johnson Grammar School, Kokapet, which offers the International Baccalaureate Primary Years Programme (IB PYP). Our Mokila Campus will offer both IB and ICSE Curriculum from Academic year 2027.

Zero Tolerance Policy

JGS has a Zero Tolerance Policy for any sort of corporal punishment and child abuse. Every staff member gives a written undertaking to follow this policy before their recruitment. Any violations are strictly dealt with.

Water Quality & Safety

We conduct frequent tests of the water used for drinking and other purposes on our school campus. This ensures the health and safety of our students.

First Aid Trained Staff

All of our staff members undergo mandatory First Aid training to handle immediate emergencies and minor injuries.
