JGS, Mokila and Kokapet, believe in the comprehensive development of students. To this effect, we have incorporated facilities for students to engage in arts & crafts, music, martial arts and physical education. We are serious when it comes to the essential facilities that our students and staff require. Hence, we have incorporated the following facilities into our school campuses.

CCTV Surveillance

We are very serious about our children’s safety. Every nook and corner of JGS Mokila and Kokapet campuses are strategically covered by high-quality CCTV cameras. This includes classrooms, hallways, playgrounds and other areas of the school.

Security Guards

We have appointed well-trained, highly experienced and child-sensitive male and female security guards on the school campus. You can definitely feel assured that your child is totally safe on the JGS Mokila and Kokapet campuses.

Health Checkups

JGS, Mokila and Kokapet have tied up with Health Basix, a nationally acclaimed school health provider, to conduct annual health checkups for our students and staff. A doctor on call is available all the time, and a full-time nurse can attend to minor injuries and health issues.

Transportation

Well-maintained air-conditioned buses equipped with CCTVs, GPS, Real-time tracking, and speed governors are used to transport our students. Also, sensitised bus staff have been appointed to monitor and ensure the safety of our students as per government guidelines.

Amphitheatre

Our spectacular semicircular amphitheatres, with tiers of seating on both sides, are the heart of JGS, Mokila and Kokapet. Our students exhibit their individual talents and do cultural performances here.

Library

Our well-stocked library is on par with international standards and contains fiction, non-fiction and reference material for our students. It’s cosy, conducive and creatively designed to inspire and initiate our young readers into a lifelong reading habit.

Playzone

Our synthetic play areas, like the basketball court and running track on the campus terrace, promote fun and physical activities along with hula hoops, slides and cones. Students develop social skills by socialising, bonding and making beautiful memories here.

Learning Aids

Every classroom at JGS, Mokila and Kokapet, are equipped with digital boards to make learning an enriching and enduring experience.
