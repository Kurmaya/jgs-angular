JGS & its philosophy

JGS is an international school that focuses on conceptual learning. We currently admit students from the Kindergarten level to the Grade 5 level. The JGS framework ensures a solid foundation for our students that contributes to their overall development ultimately.

What to expect at JGS Kokapet

At Johnson Grammar School, we embrace global standards along with Indian values. They make learning exciting and induce responsibility in students at the same time. As this resonates with our school’s vision.

Studying at Johnson Grammar School "Kokapet" will ensure every child a choice and an opportunity to be exposed to the best of global curriculums that align with the school's vision.

The world is constantly changing, and having a front foot in learning is essential. Johnson Grammar School focuses on teaching young minds to respond to challenges & opportunities quickly and to become active participants in their learning.

What’s the aim of JGS Kokapet?

The world today needs empowering individuals who can voice their opinions and make efforts to bring about change while being open and accepting of other opinions. We believe that these types of individuals will be the world's future leaders. They will highlight the importance of caring, empathy and knowledge in all elements of life.

The subject areas introduced in our school

  • Language
  • Social Studies
  • Mathematics
  • Arts
  • Science
  • Physical Education, Personal, and Social skills training

Through these subjects, students at JGS will learn to accept and appreciate the importance of conceptual understanding. The learning process always stands out from the rest for its ability to make students competent learners with self-driven skills that will enable them to lead a life filled with valuable learnings.
